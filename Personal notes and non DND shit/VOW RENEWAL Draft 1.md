All those who know Hannah and Zach (presumably all of you) know what an easy job they have given me. They have asked me to describe what makes them special. Ive picked two things from a very long list.

When I am playing a game I want to kick their ass. When I am hungry I want a seat at their table. I am honored to be standing up here again with them in ways I cant describe. About a year into their relationship they were over for some family thing. I cant really remember. What I can recollect was the two casually kissing in our living room in front of god and everyone. Two people who love the intimacy they share and aren't afraid to make that clear. Its refreshing to see them take this simple joy in daily affirmation of what they mean to each other and It bodes well for the future they share.

The second thing that marks them is an almost strategic commitment to companionship. Molding is a thing that just happens, spend enough time with someone and you might just become indistinguishable. Never did I think I would have gotten so many anime recommendations from this one. What I think separates the wheat from the chaff here is how hannah and zach both talk about this process. Many of us have seen these two in turbulent times, times that they have felt loss, times that they have searched for direction, times of change. But they both know that while it can be uncomfortable, change is inevitable. Support is one thing but hannah and zach have advocated with such excitment, who they will be linked to, they make room for the other to grow, and wait patiently to meet this new person they will find when the dust settles.
