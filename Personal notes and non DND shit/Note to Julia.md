Hey,
Long time since we've spoken! And we aren't really even speaking now! Forgive a cliche parting note, maybe everyone was right with what they said about love and grief.

Its been nearly two months since we split, I think the finality of this is beginning to take. Im scared to not know you anymore, I don't know how much sleep you're not getting, I don't hear you making a quick breakfast before class, I don't see you soak up the sunshine, Im not there and I cant be. My picture of you and yours of me can only fade, become more and more abstract with time. Its hard to put to words how painful this is, to feel a such an abrupt shift in our trajectories. 

I know you will live a full life, I'm deeply saddened that I don't get to be a part of it, at least not in the way I thought I would. But so much of me understands the necessity of it. I respect the courage It must have taken to do this, I love you so I am obliged to let you find whatever it was that you were missing. I would have orbited you for this life and the next, but I cant fault you for wanting to be unbound

I miss you, I suspect ill miss you for a very long time 

sincerely,
Sam
