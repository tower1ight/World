# Part 1: Pre Middle Ages Context 
### I. The Roman Empire pg.9
##### Major points: Why did Rome fall and other lies through omission 
- Through both over expansion and insufficient administration the empire came to have no cohesive identity, no shared ideals, or material realities 
- Recovery and construction of economies of captured land left central rome with no, 
- Deconstruction of the senate and hierarchical administration 
- The role of slave labor made expansion and conquest necessary to function, no mechanization meant that there was not cost effective labor once borders had been more or less solidified 
- Transfer of power was not a stabilized process and despite reforms to make it so, leadership passed from emperor to emperor, ultimately leading to military selected government and excessive bribes  
- Near the end constant waring and "barbarian" invasions left the empire with no cohesion, no money, and no defenses
- Corruption and repeated sacking debased the currency 
##### Diocletian and Constantine 
- Diocletian attempts to stabilize the government in the wake of augustines pseudo-republic by formalizing empirical power and deifying the position of emperor. 
- Reorganizes empire into two (east and west > prefectures (4 total) > dioceses (12 in total) > provinces > civates), reorganizes the military emphasizing hirearchy, appoints a co-emperor that both appoint their sons successors 
- the succession system doesnt take initially and Constantine's pick (his son) wins out as primary emperor and appoints his two sons, then we go back to co-emperors, one in the west, one in the east
##### Economic, Social, and Cultural Life
- A bungled and disorganized taxation system based a survey ordered by Diocletian left lots of room for corruption and the richest to pay proportionally the least, following emperors tried but failed to substantively change this (they did not try to change the tax imbalance of aristocracy, they remained tax exempt)
- More and more intention to control taxation and remove corruption lead to hereditary and compulsory labor! The middle class shrinks as the weight of taxation increases 
- In the west where less wealth and resources are present, society and and aristocracy slowly grow to be more ruralized, functioning on independent and relatively
- self sufficient keeps, this begins to set the stage for the middle ages
##### Quote Repository
- "The simplest reason that can be advanced for the fall of the roman imperial structure in the west is that the empire was just not rich enough or productive enough to support both the armies needed to defend the frontiers, and the bureaucracy needed to provide ordered central government. The eastern part of the empire had much greater resources, and the east survived" pg 14
