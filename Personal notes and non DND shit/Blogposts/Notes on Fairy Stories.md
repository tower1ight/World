Though far from an expert ive found myself writing about the fey for ttrpgs for some time now. I could be looking in the wrong places but as far as I can tell most official books fail to give useful direction on adventure structure when it comes to residents of Faerie. 

# The Good People 
- have minimal respect for reason and less respect for morality, "their chief occupations are feasting, fighting, and making love, and playing the most beautiful music" s
- fariy stories happen to people, the fey are conceptual, prickly and sensitive creatures, people do their best to not provoke them
	- When writing fey NPCs take care to write a core things  
	- What kind of deference and etiquette do they expect,
	- Break all rules you just made
- Fairy stories are fables, recommendations, they arent about morality but rather, supporting "the people's" dogmatic ideas about what is done and what is not done. that judgement does not come from the fairies themselves, it comes from god the narrator.
	- Have people observe and display "best practices"
	- Maybe even have a non fey NPC tell a short story about the fey, bonus points if its a kid relaying something their parent told them.
