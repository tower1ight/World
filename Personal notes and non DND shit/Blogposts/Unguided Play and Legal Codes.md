 # what is it

These days I feel that we as a community have successfully put murder hobos away A relic of a bygone age, but realistically this seems to have been system changed. Anecdotally most goups use group leveling or xp gain, and all the incentive for xp farming has really gone away. 

You've got reading comprehension, you've read the title, we are talking about legal codes today, and this is where I tell you that a legal code will codify this more thoughtful and enlightened moment in ttrpgs. WRONG, adding a legal code to your game will NOT pacify your players, if anything it will enhance illegality. What we do with a legal code is establish clear and thematic boundaries and consequences to behavior. 

The overwhelming majority of fantasy settings are medieval, which makes the verbiage of "legal code" questionable, legal codes existed at the time but almost all of them are continuations of roman civics, what we often have in these setting are agreements between sources of power. The lord expects military service from their vassals, however a knights other obligations demand that they were only to participate in an offensive campaign for 40 days or so, possibly more if they were being fed and housed. 

What we want to do my establishing a "legal code" is to translate the important concepts, assumptions, and perspectives of the culture our players are immersed in. The key idea is to develop a clear ideas about how power is divided and distributed, and what agreements arise between those powers
