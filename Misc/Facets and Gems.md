Thimble made circles, leaning on an elbow planted in their sleeping mat, adding lazy deviations in a central spiral that they both knew well. Stone's skin was taught and lightly spotted, hardened by a deep tan and rough-spun. It rose and feel smoothly as she went on in her hard quiet baritone, it had a rasp to that thimble had liked since they were little. With her memory drifting to more playful times thimble lightened her touch as she got closer to the apex of the rune. Callused fingers brushing hair between stones broad shoulders, eliciting a waver and a glare that thimble loved more than her voice. but no, stone wasn't turning and and the glare she was hoping for never came. This happened, stone would talk, less truthfully than thimble would have liked, but in a half hour she would be ready, and they would have another talk about their future. About Gods Veil and why they could never make a life there. Bum bum ... bum bum. She tensed. They both did, the same beat taking over and commanding their rhythm. bum bum. Thimble shivered, skin suddenly cold at a noise she *had* quitted, choked out and buried.  Stone twisted under their old blanket turning to face thimble above her, deep green eyes searching, so focused so solid, forming something like pity or regret or anger at least thats what thimble thought it was. Stone was gone in the morning.
