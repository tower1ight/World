The Circle of Blood follows the tenants of the blood pheonix, through blood we are born and without blood we die. The price for power is sacrifice, and only through sacrifice can we be reborn.


  
**Blood feature:** Bonus blood damage-  All bleed stacks you suffer or inflict are all 1d6 instead of 1d4. You always have the spell “Bleed” prepared, this does not count against your number of known spells.
  
**2nd lvl Feature:** Bleeding for a cause- When you cast a leveled spell, suffer one stack of “Bleed”. As a bonus action, you can cause an ally within 60ft of you to be healed equal to the amount of dmg you took from your bleed stacks, pre mitigation. (ex: if you have resistance to necrotic damage and would suffer 20 bleed damage, now down to 10 damage, your ally is healed for 20hp.) Instead of healing an ally, you can alternatively cause an enemy within 60ft to suffer the same amount of necrotic damage. You can use this effect a number of times equal to your wisdom modifier per long rest. Spend an action to negate all stacks of bleed taken in this way.

Bleed! Bleed is a status effect given by the “Bleed” spell. While under the effect of “Bleed”  a creature takes 1d4 necrotic damage per stack of bleed at the start of their turn. This effect lasts for 1 minute. If another stack of bleed is applied before the 1 minute expires, the cooldown is refreshed on all stacks and the timer resets. The bleed condition can be cured via lesser restoration, greater restoration, regeneration. Paladin Lay on Hands, or other such abilities which remove poisons. This would be an offshoot of the “poison” condition. Creatures that have immunity to poison are immune to bleed. Creatures that have resistance take half damage from “bleed” whilst creatures with vulnerability take double damage from “bleed”.


1st level: Bleed. creature within 60ft starts to bleed (if they can) creature suffers 1d4 necrotic damage at the start of its turn, can stack. 

- User takes 1d4 necrotic damage upon use.
- When upcast adds additional stack of bleed per level and user takes additional 1d4 per level.
- See “bleed” condition for more details.
    

  

3rd level: blood harvest. Every creature that is affected by Bleed within 30ft of the caster must make a con save or take 4d6 necrotic damage, the user heals for half of the damage dealt. A successful save halves the damage. 

- User takes 3d4 damage upon uses
- Cannot be upcast.
    


3rd level: Armor of Blood: The user forms all of their bleed stacks into a protective coating around themselves. The user gains 5 temp Hp per stack of bleed at the time of casting. While the temp HP persists, and melee attack against the caster causes the attacker to gain the number of bleed stacks on the caster.

- User takes 3d4 damage upon use. (+1d4 per upcast level)
    
- If upcast, the temp Hp per stack increases by 2 per upcast lvl.
