##### Description
The chaparral region is defined by a dry climate and distance from [[Xylaan|Xylaan's]] heart, a valley between the foothills of xylaan's Northern mountain range, [[Tani Peaks]], and the [[The Talons|Talons]], this region receives a proportionally small amount of connection to the network or the sugar layer.

Brittle and colorful, thin tall grass stretches throughout the biome, population spikes around large groves of brushlike trees that assemble around streams and the base of mountains
