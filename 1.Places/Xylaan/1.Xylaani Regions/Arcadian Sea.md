##### Description
 the Arcadian sea is an enormous lake nestled against a finger of the tallons that curves around xylaan's southern edge. Home to the [[Arcadian Court]] it holds the wealth of [[Xylaan]], a massive stockpile of [[Ichor|sugar]] stored in liquid and solid form to preserve its stability. Immiscible in water and much heavier, the [[Ichor|sugar]] sits below 20 ft of clear water.
