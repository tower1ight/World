The germ is an extension of the [[mycelium|network]], and further, [[Xylaan]]. It is the piece of [[xylaan]] that takes root in individuals and allows them their connection to it. Growing according to psionic capacity and metabolism. 

It does not give without payment, It will allow its host to break down, or in the case of photosynthetic beings, produce, [[Ichor]]. These abilities provide the host with massive stores of energy allowing them to grow to sizes well beyond those we would expect from any other ecosystem.

The physical location within the body that the germ plants itself can vary in interesting ways. However, most common is in close proximity to the brain. All [[Xylaan|Xylaani]] native species have a predisposed location for the germ to sit. Not native organisms will find that the details of where a germ plants itself can be gruesome.
