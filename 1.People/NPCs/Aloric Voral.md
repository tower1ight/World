Ganek's foot scuffed the ground and her balance shifted, quickly pulling her right foot from a gully that had doomed her and planting heavy boots in her previous gait as though moving backwards in time. In slow and practiced movement she stilled her breath and checked her surroundings, moving carefully to not tear the glue she had hastily applied to the six inch gash above her hip.  That one was stupid, Ganek had taken that hit for Gabriela, dead now anyway. This new wound was layered on two she had healed years before, part of her hoped the resulting stiffness would put her out of commission. With debts paid, and rent covered, and It was hard to not rethink her line of work after seeing that girl holding her guts in her hands. Gan would give her her rights at the surface, for some reason she was most sentimental when she was desperate.

She was in a tight hallway, the maze, an infamous rotting mass of impossibly tall bookshelves. It was at the center of the stacks atrium and the shortest route you could take if you wanted to get pretty much anywhere in this place, naturally it was stupid dangerous, to be avoided at all costs.

 Finishing her look she kneeled to see the trap, this was a new one and despite her situation it was worth investigating. However, with a pop and a sizzle her torch was out and the open dark of the cavern closed around her. Silently cursing her fingers found her last torch and the starter in her pocket. With the light back on she moved quickly, she found a grove in the rotting hardwood and planted the torch. There was always uncertainty about this place, but Ganek was one of the oldest living delvers in the guild. She knew how much time she had, and it was always, ALWAYS worth investigating a new trap. She and her compatriots were in an arms race with this place, and if they stopped learning, she would soon be joining Gabriela. Fingers in the gully, she lifted it slightly, seeing a mechanism under it click, A door then! lets see how big. She stepped back and cautiously triggered the latch. Instantly the floor before her folded in. She waited a moment, and edged up to the ledge, she picked up her torch and raised it for more details. In vain, very unfortunate. Just about to back away she heard a squelch and something hit her hard across the shoulders. Years of this work allowed her to keep herself from falling over, bracing herself against the wall and keeping her torch upright lit. She struggled as the thing moved and undulated over her back. An ooze, fuck, this was not good. Something whispered softly in the back of her mind trying to ease her muscles but It went unheard. She fell back on the thing, making it splat against the wood. Fumbling for a dagger one hand she quickly flipped and pinned the doughy creature with both knees. The mass off clear stringy flesh and fat right beneath her belly shot out, but Gan had anticipated that, she twisted hard, letting the punch fly past her, but tearing her side clean open. She roared, only furry and hate behind her eyes, as she plunging her dagger into a knot of muscle and twisted. Burning clear fluid shot out of the wound covering her and mixing with the stream of blood pouring from her abdomen. An exhaltant cry almost like pleasure shrieked through her head, and she froze, pure confusion breaking her focus. With an open window the creature shot out limbs from under her and grabbed the ledge, sending them both flying. Fuck fuck fuck. and blinding pain as her face hit the corner. Gushing hot blood and blinding pain, she swallowed something hard.
