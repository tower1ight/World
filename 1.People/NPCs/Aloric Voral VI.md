Teloric Monarch and perpetrator of the [[False Ascension]]
Morphed into [[MoundRank]]

Aloric VI is a queen, though she meanders and picks up diversions she has always held to that as her greatest drive. She is not a kind woman and she speaks with no honey but her insight has a way of making neither of those things relevant. She, above all in her line has understood the leverage of her crown and her church, enough to see jaws closing in. She set out to free her people from [[Tel'Voric]]'s war, and in searching she saw one answer. With the allegiance of her people and a holy office she sought to ride to his level, take his oxygen and chart a new path. That is until she failed to ascend, halted by the gods wrath, wrath that could not be deceived or thwarted with mortal tricks. She was contorted melted into a puddle with her followers, hiding in deep hole and stewing personal failures. A foul holy creature with no where to put her rage.
