# Variants

##### Weavers
Weavers are quaradpedal spider like constructs about the size of a human fist. Unlike other modrones their (animating threads) can be stretched and drawn out of themselves by their needle like feet. They cast with these threads, specifically designed to maintain protections. They often work in swarms weaving, closing gaps in the libraries protections.
