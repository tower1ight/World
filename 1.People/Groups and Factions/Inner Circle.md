# Scenes From A Coup

##### Cuppa
A lean dwarf sits on the edge of a saddle, A sort of detachable chair that he's bolted to a wireframe running across an inscrutable workshop. Something within it clicks slowly as he leans back , examining a mesh of spinning gears, ungently flicking a leaver a leaver the set rearranges seamlessly twisting and inverting in perfect sync. 

*Knock*

Ursk stopped just shy shouting at the knocker to come in, he grunted and cleared days of gravel and phlegm and whatever else one accumulates in their throat when they haven't spoken in a week. "Come in", and the door opened, revealing a warforged, well polished dark wood and gold accents, the figure stood 9 feet tall in olive robes. He switched arms and closed the door slowing just before contact with the frame. Moving swiftly and quietly, Renta moved to the small kitchen area and began filling a heavy kettle, ringed fingers clinking every surface, the dwarf liked his metal. Looking over his shoulder he eyed the project Ursk had gotten lost in again, 

"new box?" 
"Nope, same as last time you saw it"
"casings changed, and oh" renta's eyes readjusted telescoping some what
"Yes, new guide wires, past ones keep burning up on me"
"You gonna tell me what its for"
"nope . . ."
"Only fair that I keep asking"
"Always on the table"
"I know, Maybe I just don't consider myself that curious" Renta lit the burner and placed the kettle
"Curious enough to keep asking"
"You mistake a polite interest in my colleague's work, Its very possible you were pursuing your own project"
"This is my project"
Renta moved to sit on the bench by the rooms table, and was forced to relocate several neat stacks of blueprints. 
"On that shelf" 
Caught trying to place them on a nearby cabinet, Renta rolled his eyes and trekked across his friends workshop, and with some flourish placed them on the shelf Ursk had indicated.

Finally sitting at the table, palms laid on its face, a bright and earthy aroma wafted from the brewed tea in front of the two. 

"No questions for me? No polite offers to hear of my work"
The dwarf scoffed "That is not politeness, besides were not in your lab, they require experiencing to understand, technical drivel would cheapen it"
"Fair point, an understandable opinion to be sure. There is much to say around anima without speaking of it directly"
"well im direct, and you sir are not interested in talking about that either"
"Oh Im not?"
"No you want to ask me about Him"
"Not always about you"
"In this case it is"
"ok fine, fair, noted"
". . ."
"whatever . . . I just have to know what youre getting out of this, Hes not a well loved drake"
"No drake is, but if not loved, most respect him around here"
"Yes but he shows his character constantly, the elves hate him, think hes ambitious even for a dragon"
". . ."
"And hes not exactly on excellent terms with the boss either, who knows how hes still a steward after he burned those books in the stacks"
"Hes got a temper thats right, and hes still a steward cause hes good, very good, not just with his lore, he has a magnetism to him, its hard to deny"
" . . . a magnetism"
"yes"
"Ury"
"Thats not why im doing this, but I, he has a vision of what this place could be that's compelling, I like working on something concrete. Deliberate action with deliberate goals. Thats hard to come by here"
"But you realize that a vision of this place cant work right, this place forms itself, the libraries will, It has given use jurisdiction but"
"Gods can be swayed, they can be convinced of a path. Especially if the alternative is where were going"
