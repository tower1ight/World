A physical object imbued with unchanneled [[soul]] to the point of consciousness!

While they are magic items, Mommets are not to be confused with a magic item with sentience as a byproduct of their function. Mommets serve ulterior function, they may have a function (often for information storage), but they are not designed so much as they are born. 

Though few of sightings of them have been recorded, existing accounts have described them having a overwhelming presence capable of overriding ones most basic understandings of the world.


Manny of the known mommets are located within the [[The Great Library]]
Few know of their existance in the hierarchy but a few of these are possesed by the [[Cult of the Library]] and obtain the highest degree of knowledge within their ranks
