# [[Seraphs|Seraphine]] battery material 
A somewhat opaque reddish brown material, synthesized in large reactors and poured into molds, in a process that emulates [[Goblins|Goblin]] [[mold]] shaping practices, to form an incredibly light and flexible material capable reforming their original shape. However, It is its similarities to seraphine physiology that it make it so crucial to life in the [[{Crown}]]. While it lacks the mental acuity of the seraphs, they created it to be even better than they are are filtering out sugar from the air and storing it as usable energy. One of the most notable applications of this light battery material is as hull coating. The [[skiffs]] that define the [[Seraphs|seraphine]] trade are constructed almost entirely with this material, ensuring consistent and nearly indefinite flight times in the [[Ichor#Curling Mists]]
