# The Importance of Public Works
Everyone is quick to say cities are living and breathing things and change is important to illustrate that life, its somewhat simple to drop in npc's feelings about recent shifts or movements in political power, or a oncoming threat to the cities populous. but often, at least in my experience, this can feel rather flat and leave the players mind as soon as possible.

Large iconic buildings are great ways to represent a cities history, culturally, politically, economically, in the same way new or in progress building projects are tools for communicating the present and future of that city
