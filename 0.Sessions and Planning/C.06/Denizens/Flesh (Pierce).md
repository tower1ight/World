Ive discussed this in great detail but I think I understand it better right now. Allow me to be melodramatic! I think Ive spent a lot of my life secretly hoping for misfortune, often in pursuit of getting my needs met, but a layer under that is a yearning for emotional volatility. I have always secretly wanted to break down in hysterics, to tear my hair and cry out in despair, to completely loose myself to grief. I don't think I want an audience for this, in fact I don't think I could ever have this fantasy experience in the presence of anyone. Im not seeking validation. Im not seeking disavowal. Im seeking attachment so profound that loss of that thing might as well be loss of myself. Im seeking proof that I am alive and am wounded by loss. 

I am ruminating on the following idea. If I cannot sum up hysterical, panicked, and irrational feeling for the loss of Julia, then I must not have truly loved her. If this doesn't do it then what will? Why cant I feel? Am I bound to a body or mind that is just less emotional? Why do I feel next to nothing? Why do I give a fuck that dishes are in the sink? why am I doing my laundry? Why is anything important right now I just lost the love of my life?
