**Atrium: Camp** 
A thicker smell of bodies pervades this place allowing the mildew to take a backseat for once. The place is a large rectangular section of the atrium with its tables and mess organized in neat rows of stalls. Merchants ply their wares for exorbitant prices, Guilds have camped out in the drier sections of the floor, their mages scribbling on the floor in small groups, attempting to solve something. A large table at sits on the eastern wall against a large cleared cleared out bookshelf holding many alchemical jars and supplies, [[The Butcher]] wields a long, thin, blade, separating a warebat from its bladder. 

- This place is tolerated to some extent as a lifeline to guilds flirting with death. However, sale of any goods must be performed in gold, the guild must have its due, any indication that you have subverted guild taxes or bartered for goods is grounds for expulsion and contract termination 
- players may find a safe place to camp. no meat grinder unless as a direct action form the [[MoundRank]]
