Structure
This floor's layout is not set, and is generated as the players and GM place rooms, the guild setting rooms in front of them, and the GM placing rooms behind them. Each time the floor is entered, it has a GM-tracked HP bar that gets ticked down with each encounter until it is at zero, and the players may place the dungeon lord's chamber.

When the players enter a doorway, they draw three cards, these cards correspond to Places and Powers. Most of these will be places, as in numbered rooms with typical room descriptions. Powers, on the other hand, represent the floor's instability and malleable nature, each power gives its user the ability to manipulate the existing dungeon layout. Once drawn, the players select a card to place or use. If they choose a room, they enter that room, if they choose a power, they activate the card's description, walk back into the room they had just exited, the GM will draw on the meatgrinder table once more to show that the room has changed, and the door behind them will vanish. Of the two cards remaining, the GM selects one and places it anywhere in the dungeon that they wish (including between two connected rooms) so long as the floor remains contiguous, the last card is then discarded and may not be placed this delve. Finally, secret room connections are set, as in 503 will always link to 508. If both are not already on the map, then players may walk through a secret entrance and place its link wherever they choose.

Here are a few of my concerns

- Danger: A changing and moving dungeon is a treacherous one, with the GM's ability to put rooms between the players and their exit, the guild may get in over their head fast.
- Lowest level: Thematically, this dungeon gimmick works best with the themes and atmosphere of my dungeon's lowest level. This means players will likely have traveled through two floors at least and will be very light on resources.
- Unfamiliar: I can see this mechanic working best with knowledge of the rooms. If my players are taking notes, they may know to avoid room 507 or favor 515. I have not run a full-scale campaign of HMTW, so realistically, how much time do guilds spend on a floor as deep as fifth?

Example powers

- Not quite yet: Reserve this power and use it in a future drafting stage to hold on to a room for later placement
- Poof: Eliminate a room from the dungeons layout
- Run it back: The guild may walk through any open door within a 4-room distance of their current location
